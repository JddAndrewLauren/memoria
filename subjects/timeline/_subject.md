## Match

An entry under Timeline represents a period or date range in the archive's chronology.

## Hazards

Do not merge two periods on the strength of a shared label alone; a partial date may belong to more than one period. Contemporaneous and retrospective dating for the same period can disagree - do not silently prefer one.

## Audit questions

Does the passage place an event outside its settled period?
Does it assume a chronology the archive does not support?
