## Match

An entry under Events represents a discrete happening the archive records.

## Hazards

Do not merge two events described from different vantage points without corroboration; the same happening can be reported under different names or dates by different sources. Do not conflate a recurring kind of event with one specific instance of it.

## Audit questions

Does the passage contradict a settled fact about this event?
Does it place the event out of step with the entry's account?
